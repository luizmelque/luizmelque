I enjoy understanding how systems work, questioning requirements, identifying risks early and helping teams deliver reliable software before defects reach production.

My journey into technology came after experiences in completely different fields, and that taught me something I carry into Quality Assurance every day:

> **Quality is built through discipline, communication and attention to detail.**

Today I'm focused on becoming the kind of QA Engineer that development teams trust not only to find bugs, but to improve software quality throughout the entire development process.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/luizmelque/qa-design-system/main/headers/philosophy.svg" alt="Engineering Principles"/>

These principles guide the way I approach software quality.

- Quality starts with clear requirements.
- Testing reduces risk, it doesn't prove perfection.
- Good documentation is part of the product.
- Communication is one of the strongest QA skills.
- Bugs are symptoms. Understanding their causes creates real quality.
- Automation should simplify maintenance, not increase complexity.
- Continuous improvement is more valuable than perfection.
